<p class="eyebrow">What we mean by 3D</p>

## A dimension is a degree of freedom.

<div class="prose two-column">
<p>Three-dimensional computing gave us a way to represent space. We use <em>3D</em> more broadly: as a question about the coordinates an intelligent system can perceive, manipulate, and invent.</p>
<p>Those coordinates may be spatial, temporal, semantic, physical, perceptual, or higher-dimensional. The useful question is not how many dimensions a system has, but which dimensions make a new kind of thought or action possible.</p>
</div>
