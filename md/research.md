# Research

AI3D explores how humans can create, edit, and program 3D worlds with AI.

Our research focuses on the representations and interactions between human spatial intuition and machine intelligence: what we give to an AI, what it gives back, and what becomes possible in between.

Much of this work takes the form of working systems.

---

## Selected Research

### AI3D Canvas with Spatial Creation Agents
**Space → AI → Space**  
*2026*

AI3D Canvas explores agents that create within spatial context.

A Spatial Creation Agent receives a scan or spatial model of a room and uses that context to determine what to create, where to place it, and how it should relate to the existing environment.

Rather than generating isolated assets, the agent participates in the composition of a shared spatial world.

**ECCV 2026 — Demonstration**

---

### AI3D Co-Create with AI3D Render
**3D ↔ AI ↔ Generative Media**  
*2025*

AI3D Co-Create explores iterative creation between a human and multimodal AI using 3D as a shared representation.

Rough geometry can communicate spatial intent. AI can transform or elaborate that intent. The resulting imagery and geometry can become material for another iteration.

AI3D Render extends this approach by using 3D scenes, cameras, composition, and animation as controls for generative image and video.

Instead of specifying a scene entirely through language, we can show an AI what we mean spatially.

**ACM SIGGRAPH 2025 — Appy Hour**  
**CVPR 2025 — AI3D Render, Best Demo Honorable Mention**

[Publication](https://doi.org/10.1145/3721260.3733979) · [CVPR Demo](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2025/BestPapersDemos)

### AI3D VolumeMatic
**AI → Spatial Applications**  
*2024*

AI3D VolumeMatic investigates generative AI as a way to construct interactive spatial applications.

Rather than stopping at the generation of an individual model or image, the work asks how AI-generated assets, behaviors, and interactions can become components of an executable spatial experience.

The object of generation becomes not simply a 3D asset, but a world that can do something.

**ACM SIGGRAPH 2024**

---

### AI3D Sculpt
**3D → 3D**  
*2024*

AI3D Sculpt explores direct spatial manipulation as an interface to generative 3D.

Text and images are useful representations of intent, but neither completely describes shape.

A rough 3D construction can communicate information directly: proportion, orientation, topology, spatial relationships, and perspective become part of the input to generation.

The model is not only the result.

The model can be the prompt.

**ECCV 2024 — Demonstration**

[ECCV Demo](https://eccv.ecva.net/virtual/2024/demonstration/2781)

---

### AI3D Desktop
**Multimodal → 3D**  
*2024*

AI3D Desktop explores what a desktop environment might become when generative 3D is a native computational primitive.

The system combines text-to-3D, image-to-3D, 3D-to-3D, and direct spatial manipulation inside a skeuomorphic XR environment.

Instead of treating a generative model as an isolated prompt box, AI3D Desktop makes generation one operation within a persistent creative workspace.

**CVPR 2024 — Demonstration**  
**ACM SIGGRAPH 2024 — Appy Hour**

[CVPR Demo](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/virtual/2024/demonstration/32144)

### Napkinmatic 3D
**Drawing → 3D**  
*2023*

Draw something on paper. Turn it into 3D.

Napkinmatic 3D investigates ordinary physical drawing as an interface to generative 3D.

A napkin, notebook, receipt, or other available surface can become a bridge between human visual intuition and machine-generated geometry.

The work asks a recurring AI3D question:

Why should a person first translate an idea into the representation preferred by a computer?

**ACM SIGGRAPH 2023 — Real-Time Live!**

[Publication](https://doi.org/10.1145/3588430.3597253)

## The Napkinmatic Lineage

Napkinmatic began with a broader question:

**What happens when computation can begin with whatever representation a person already has in front of them?**

Rather than requiring people to enter a specialized authoring environment, Napkinmatic treats ordinary drawings and physical artifacts as computational interfaces.

This research developed through several systems and publications, including:

**Napkinmatic**  
Physical drawing as computational input.

**Napkinmatic: Ubiquitous Pocket AGI**  
Exploring ubiquitous multimodal AI available through ordinary objects and environments.

**Napkinmatic 3D**  
Extending the interface from drawing into generative geometry.

The lineage anticipates a broader theme throughout AI3D:

**the representation used to communicate with intelligence does not have to be language.**

## A Continuing Question

These projects can be understood as transformations between representations.

**Drawing → computation**  
Napkinmatic

**Drawing → 3D**  
Napkinmatic 3D

**Multimodal → 3D**  
AI3D Desktop

**3D → 3D**  
AI3D Sculpt

**AI → spatial applications**  
AI3D VolumeMatic

**3D → generative image & video**  
AI3D Render

**Human ↔ 3D ↔ AI**  
AI3D Co-Create

**Space → agent → space**  
AI3D Canvas with Spatial Creation Agents

The underlying question remains:

> **What should the interface between human spatial intuition and machine intelligence be?**

3D is not only an output format.

It can be an input.

It can be a prompt.

It can be an intermediate representation.

It can encode constraints.

It can describe a world.

It can provide context to an agent.

And it can become a shared computational space in which humans and machines create together.

---

## Beyond Three Dimensions

3D began with space.

It doesn't end there.

A representation determines which relationships become easy to express and which transformations become easy to compute.

Our broader research investigates representations, transformations, interfaces, and systems through which intelligence can operate across spatial and other structured dimensions.

Sometimes the result is a model.

Sometimes it is an interface.

Sometimes it is an experiment whose purpose is simply to make a previously abstract idea executable.

We build to find out.

## Field Building

Research does not happen only through papers.

AI3D Foundation also works on the infrastructure through which AI-for-3D research can be shared, compared, reproduced, and developed.

This has included organizing the **AI for 3D Special Interest Group at ACM SIGGRAPH**, developing experiments around benchmarks and shared representations, and teaching hands-on approaches to incorporating state-of-the-art generative AI into 3D applications.

### AI for 3D Special Interest Group

An ongoing gathering at ACM SIGGRAPH connecting researchers, artists, developers, and practitioners working across generative AI and 3D.

### Implementing SOTA Generative AI Pipelines in Your 3D Application

A hands-on ACM SIGGRAPH 2025 Lab exploring practical integration of contemporary generative models into interactive 3D systems.

[Publication](https://doi.org/10.1145/3721251.3734070)
