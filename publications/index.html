2026

AI3D Canvas with Spatial Creation Agents

AI3D Canvas lets people sketch, trace, place, stretch, compose, and otherwise manipulate forms directly in freeform space. It translates that spatial expression into the representation an AI model requires, then returns the result to an editable 3D world: the human works in space while the machinery of representation happens underneath.

Spatial Creation Agents extend this shared canvas. They receive geometry, objects, relationships, and composition as context, then create in relation to the world already there—moving from AI as generator to AI as a participant in a continuing spatial creation environment.

2025

AI3D Co-Create with AI3D Render

Yosun Chang, Michael Gold, Caramel @corgi.cam Chang.

Co-Create treats 3D as a shared representation for iterative creation between people and multimodal AI: rough geometry communicates spatial intent, model output informs the next action, and the loop remains editable. AI3D Render makes scenes, cameras, composition, and animation controls for generative image and video, so creators can show an AI what they mean spatially.

2024

AI3D VolumeMatic

AI3D VolumeMatic investigates generative AI for interactive spatial applications. Generated assets, behaviors, and interactions become components of an executable spatial experience: the object of generation is not simply a 3D asset, but a world that can do something.

AI3D Sculpt

AI3D Sculpt explores direct spatial manipulation as an interface to generative 3D. A rough construction communicates proportion, orientation, topology, perspective, and spatial relationships directly: the model is not only the result; it can be the prompt.

AI3D Desktop makes generative 3D a native operation in a persistent creative workspace, combining text-to-3D, image-to-3D, 3D-to-3D, and direct spatial manipulation inside a skeuomorphic XR environment.

2023

Napkinmatic 3D

Napkinmatic 3D turns an ordinary drawing on a napkin, notebook, receipt, or another available surface into a bridge from human visual intuition to generated geometry. It asks why a person should first translate an idea into the representation preferred by a computer.
