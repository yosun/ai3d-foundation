I

What we mean by 3D

A dimension is a degree of freedom.

Three-dimensional computing gave machines coordinates for representing space.

We use 3D as the beginning of a larger question: what are the degrees of freedom through which an intelligent system can perceive, generate, transform, and act?

Some are ordinary spatial dimensions. Others emerge from time, geometry, representation, perception, or learned structure.

The important question is not how many dimensions a system has. It is what becomes possible when we can compute through them.

III

Why independent

Ideas should be able to exist before they have permission.

Some questions do not fit neatly inside a discipline, product roadmap, grant category, or institution.

Independence lets us follow a question across those boundaries and build whatever experiment is necessary to test it.

We publish what can be shared. We demonstrate what can be built. We prefer working systems to claims of ownership.
